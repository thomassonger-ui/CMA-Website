Our Story

Built by educators, for educators

Cole Middleton Advisors was founded on a simple belief — that every institution deserves the same level of expert guidance that large universities take for granted. Since 2012, we've worked alongside presidents, provosts, deans, and boards to solve their hardest challenges.

"We may not be right for every school — but what we have collectively is over 100 years of experience managing and growing educational institutions. We show up, we listen, and we deliver."— Dr. J. Angley, Founder & Principal Advisor

How We Work

A proven four-step engagement process

No cookie-cutter playbooks. Every engagement begins with deep listening and ends with measurable results.

01

Discovery

An in-depth institutional assessment reviewing your accreditation status, financials, enrollment trends, and strategic goals.

02

Strategy

A customized action plan with clear priorities, timelines, and ownership — built around your unique institutional context.

03

Implementation

Hands-on support during execution — coaching, documentation, and stakeholder communication every step of the way.

04

Stewardship

Ongoing monitoring, quarterly reviews, and proactive adjustments to keep your institution growing long after engagement ends.
